Manufacturing Downtime Analysis

I built a one-page Power BI dashboard that shows what keeps a production line from running at full speed, who and what is behind the downtime, and what to fix first. Built on a public sample dataset.

Role
Analysis & dashboard design
Tools
Power BI · Excel
Data
Maven Analytics sample dataset (fictional data)
Type
Portfolio project

01 · The question

The line runs at 64% efficiency. Where does the downtime come from, and what should the plant manager fix first?

02 · The approach

One page, and every chart title states its finding. Four KPI cards on top, a Pareto chart to find the few causes that matter, then a breakdown by operator and by product. Colour is used only for what needs action.

03 · What the data shows

80%
of the 1,388 downtime minutes come from just 5 causes
56%
of downtime is related to operator errors
44%
efficiency on product OR-600, against 62–68% on the others

04 · What I would do first

Start with the few causes that explain most of the loss.
  • Focus on the top 5 causes. Machine adjustment (332 min), machine failure, inventory shortage, batch change and batch coding error add up to 80% of the downtime. The rest can wait.
  • Train on machine adjustment and batch change. Machine adjustment is the biggest single cause. One operator has the lowest efficiency (61%), mostly because of long batch changes (130 min).
  • Look into OR-600. Its efficiency is far below the other products, so its production issues need a root-cause check.
  • Fix CO-600 supply and reliability. It has the most downtime that no operator causes: machine failures and inventory shortages.
